EFT/Remittance FAQs

Who should I contact for B2B registration, log in, navigation or Administrator changes?

Please submit the Contact Us Email Form for questions on B2B Registration.

Questions on Admin changes please see the FAQ for Admin roles and responsibilities.

Questions on log in you can view the self-help page for Log In Assistance or submit a Contact Us Email Form.

Who should I contact if I am unable to view EFT "View Remittance"Link?

First you should contact the Admin of your business to make sure you have the correct role to access the remittance application. Users are required to have the EFT role.

You can also submit a Contact Us Email Form for questions or Contact the B2B Help Desk at 855-311-2681.

Who should I contact if I need additional information on a payment? (ex: Why did we receive this payment, I believe this payment was issued in error)

Please contact the claim handler associated to the claim. You may find the claim handler's contact information within the payment details section under Contact Name and Contact phone number columns.

When will my payments be viewable on the B2B website?

Non-Medical payments will be view-able within 1-3 days of payment date. Medical payments will be view-able within 5 days of payment date.

Why can't I see an Explanation of Review (EOR) for a payment?

Work Comp related medical payments will not include a copy of the EOR on B2B.statefarm.com. A copy of the EOR will be mailed to you for Work Comp related payments.

It is not uncommon for EORs to process 2-3 days after the payment is ready for viewing. If the missing EOR is not regarding a Work Comp claim and it has been more than 3 days, please contact the B2B Help Desk at 855-311-2681.

To request a paper or faxed copy of the EOR please contact the claim handler associated to the claim. You may find the claim handler's contact information within the payment details section under Contact Name and Contact phone number columns.

How can I obtain an EOR that is not viewable?

You may contact the claim handler to request a copy of the Explanation of Review (EOR).

Why can't I find any payments when I click on "View Remittance"?

Please click on all zip codes to view your payments.

  • You will see payment information for every zip code that has been paid under your Tax ID Number (TIN).
    • You may have to use the Ctrl-F function to "find" your zip code. Look for multiple results in the zip code.

    I have clicked "View" for all zip codes and still cannot find the missing payment?

    If you still cannot see them, please log out, delete your temporary internet files/cookies and then close and reopen your browser to log in again.

    If this does not resolve your issue, please contact the B2B Help Desk at 855-311-2681.

    How do I update my bank account information?

    Click on the "EFT Forms" link for non-medical vendors or "P&C EFT Forms" for medical vendors. Select the appropriate option applicable to your needs and follow the directions when prompted.

    *Note: Only Administrators will have access to the "EFT Forms" and/or "P&C EFT Forms" link.*

    Can I receive emails advising me of new payments?

    At this time, this is not a function of the B2B website.

    How can I update my address or Tax Id Number (TIN)?

    Please contact the B2B Help Desk at 855-311-2681.

    Where do I go to add additional users?

    Administrators may add additional users through the Profile & Settings tab (top navigation) and selecting the "User Admin" tab.
