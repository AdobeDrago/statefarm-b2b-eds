Servicer change 101

101

Used for subservicing agreements to notify State Farm that all notices for insurance policies with the included mortgagee clause should be redirected to a new servicing location but, the mortgage clause should not be changed on the insurance policy.

State Farm will redirect all electronic notices for the identified mortgagee clause (LV) to the servicing mortgagee clause (SE).
