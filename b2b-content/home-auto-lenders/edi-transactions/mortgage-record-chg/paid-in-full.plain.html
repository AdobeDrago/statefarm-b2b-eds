Loan paid in full 104

104

Used by the servicer to inform State Farm the borrower terminated their interest in the insurance policy because the loan has been prepaid.

DO NOT send this code on a Serviced Release or refinanced loan.

State Farm will remove the mortgagee clause and loan number from the insurance policy and redirect the billing to the insured.
