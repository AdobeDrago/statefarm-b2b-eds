Portfolio audit 066

066

Used by the servicer to communicate loan information to State Farm for the purpose of ensuring they are listed on the insurance policy per their requirements.

State Farm will change the loan number, billing option, and mortgagee clause on the policy if the audit meets our specifications. If requested, State Farm will return an electronic insurance verification (811).
