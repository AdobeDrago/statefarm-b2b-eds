Third party transmission schedule

The following information outlines the transmission schedule for State Farm's Mortgage and Motor Vehicle Notification Systems.

Daily Transmissions

Those receiving daily transmissions from State Farm will receive their files Monday through Friday (or Tuesday through Saturday, depending on the time of the completed billing file). Transmissions will not be sent on weekends and Holidays (noted in the schedule below) unless problems are encountered during normal hours.

Holidays

  • New Year's Day
  • Memorial Day
  • Independence Day
  • Labor Day
  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Friday after Thanksgiving Day
  • Christmas Day
