Contact Details

To help expedite your call review the hints and tips below:

  1. Have the Policy Number and Insured Name available
  2. Only three policies can be inquired on per phone call
  3. If your Company is not listed on the policy we will not be able to provide policy information
  4. Only current term policy information will be provided for Active and Terminated policies
  5. Contact the State Farm Agent for dec page requests, reason for cancellation or non-renewal or if you need to add or update your information on the policy
  6. Visit the State Farm Business to Business (B2B) website to request access to our Insurance Inquiry application
