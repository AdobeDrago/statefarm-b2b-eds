Policy information

When an exact match is made for Policy details the Policy Information screen is received. This screen displays Additional Interests (as applicable), and Policy Information such as Name, Address, Coverages, Deductibles, Companion policies, and etc.

Auto Third Party

You can only view Auto Policy Information at this time. There are no update capabilities for Auto Policies.

Mortgage Lenders

You have the option to do the following:

View Policy Information

  • Utilize the UPDATE ADDITIONAL INTERESTS button (See Additional Interest information)
  • Utilize the CREATE CERTIFICATE button to generate a Certificate of Insurance. This is only available for Condo Association policies at this time. (See Certificate of Insurance Overview)
